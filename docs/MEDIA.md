# Demo footage credits

## Real handheld demo

`real-demo.mp4` and `real-demo.gif` use footage from **Peter Cooper Jr.**,
[Red fox playing with prey in yard, Charlton MA 2025-12-26](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Red_fox_playing_with_prey_in_yard,_Charlton_MA_2025-12-26.webm),
released under the [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Changes: the first 20 seconds were resized to 1280×720 and encoded as H.264,
processed with SteadyFrame Balanced settings (0.5-second smoothing radius,
1.05× minimum crop, 1.25× maximum crop), and arranged side by side with labels.
The MP4 shows the full evaluated excerpt; the GIF previews its first eight
seconds at 10 FPS. The demo is silent. No artificial camera shake was added.

The original appears on the left and the stabilized result on the right.
The source creator does not endorse this project.

## Synthetic demo

`demo.gif` is generated by `tools/demo.py` from a reproducible synthetic scene.
It is separate from the real-footage demo.
